1. 等待离开闪屏 → 权限 → Accept/Agree & Continue（可选）

## 步骤 B — 登录后（t42 与 t42-postlogin 共用，对齐 Figma）

### 首页 → Get Cash Now 主流程（必须走完）
3. 校验 AVAILABLE CREDIT LIMIT、Interest Rate 10.49% p.a.、Tenure Up to 60 M、Processing Zero Fee、Get Cash Now
4. 校验 Our Services 六宫格与 Trusted by 10M+ Indians / 100% Secure / Instant Disbursal / Minimal Paperwork
5. 点 Get Cash Now
6. Personal Information：填 Full Name、Mobile；Date of Birth **选日 + OK**；Employment Type=Salaried → Continue
7. Document Upload：Upload PAN / Upload Aadhaar（人工）→ Continue
8. Face Verification：Take a picture（人工自拍）→ Verify Face
9. Verification Completed：**必须校验 PROFILE SCORE 得分（XX/100，如 87/100）** → Back to Home → 回 Home

### 贷款详情
10. Personal Loan 文案校验 → 返回；Business Loan 文案校验 → 返回

### Profile / Help / Account Details（个人信息+头像需测完）
11. Profile 菜单与 Delete Account? → Cancel
12. Help & Support：Service Email = business@rajwiseloanadvisor.com
13. Account Details：Change Photo（人工）→ 填 Nickname、Email → **选 Emergency Contact 并校验回显** → 收键盘 → Save changes → 返回
